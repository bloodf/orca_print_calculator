```markdown
## More plugins for your OrcaSlicer workflow

- **[Organizer](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/5bae3da1-7fcc-4cbd-ae25-53678e392d54)**: Arrange objects by their real outline and fill a build plate with more copies.
- **[Spooler](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/5c523246-d53c-44bc-a129-fe2470da6368)**: Your Spoolman spools and filament presets in OrcaSlicer, with what is left on each spool.
- **[U1 Control](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/544a836a-bcbe-4517-ae33-90f33930682f)**: Prepare and monitor Snapmaker U1 prints: toolheads, camera, files and Spoolman.

Each plugin works on its own; installed together they share data where it helps (U1 Control shows Spooler's stock; the Print Calculator uses Spooler's Spoolman address and filament presets).
```

## Português

```markdown
## Mais plugins para o seu fluxo no OrcaSlicer

- **[Organizer](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/5bae3da1-7fcc-4cbd-ae25-53678e392d54)**: Organize os objetos pelo contorno real e preencha a mesa com mais cópias.
- **[Spooler](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/5c523246-d53c-44bc-a129-fe2470da6368)**: Suas bobinas do Spoolman e os perfis de filamento no OrcaSlicer, com quanto resta em cada bobina.
- **[U1 Control](https://cloud.orcaslicer.com/app/plugins/shared-plugins/544a836a-bcbe-4517-ae33-90f33930682f)**: Prepare e acompanhe impressões na Snapmaker U1: cabeçotes, câmera, arquivos e Spoolman.

Cada plugin funciona sozinho; instalados juntos, compartilham dados onde ajuda (o U1 Control mostra o estoque do Spooler; a Print Calculator usa o endereço do Spoolman e os perfis de filamento do Spooler).
```
